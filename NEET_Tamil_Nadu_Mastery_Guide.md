# NEET-UG Mastery & Reference Guide for Tamil Nadu Aspirants

## 1. What is NEET-UG and How is it Conducted?

The **National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test (Undergraduate) [NEET-UG]**, conducted once a year (typically on the **first Sunday of May**, 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM IST) by the **National Testing Agency (NTA)**, is the single unified national medical entrance examination in India. Qualifying NEET-UG is mandatory for admission to:
- **MBBS** (Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery)
- **BDS** (Bachelor of Dental Surgery)
- **AYUSH** courses (BSMS — Siddha, BAMS — Ayurveda, BHMS — Homoeopathy, BUMS — Unani)
- **BVSc & AH** (Veterinary Sciences) and **B.Sc. Nursing** (in central institutions like JIPMER Puducherry, AIIMS, AFMC)

### Official NTA Exam Pattern (Current 180-Question Compulsory Structure)
> **Important Pattern Update:** NTA removed the COVID-era optional "Section B" (200 questions / 200 minutes) starting in 2025. The exam strictly follows the **180 compulsory questions in 180 minutes** format.

| Parameter | Official Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Conducting Body** | National Testing Agency (NTA) — `neet.nta.nic.in` |
| **Mode of Exam** | **Offline (Pen-and-Paper)** using an **OMR (Optical Mark Recognition)** bubble sheet and NTA-supplied black ballpoint pen |
| **Total Duration** | **3 Hours (180 Minutes)** — 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM IST |
| **Total Questions** | **180 Compulsory Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)** — 4 options (1, 2, 3, 4 / A, B, C, D), single correct answer |
| **Maximum Marks** | **720 Marks** |
| **Physics** | **45 Questions** — 180 Marks |
| **Chemistry** | **45 Questions** — 180 Marks (Physical, Organic, Inorganic) |
| **Biology (Botany)** | **45 Questions** — 180 Marks |
| **Biology (Zoology)** | **45 Questions** — 180 Marks |
| **Correct Response** | **+4 Marks** |
| **Incorrect Response** | **-1 Mark** (Negative Marking — 25% penalty of question value) |
| **Unattempted Question** | **0 Marks** (No penalty) |
| **Multiple Bubbles Filled** | Treated as **Incorrect (-1 Mark)**; OMR bubbles cannot be erased once darkened |

### Question Types Tested Inside the 180 MCQs
Modern NTA papers no longer ask simple one-line recall questions alone. Every mock test must train the student on all five NTA formats:
1. **Standard Conceptual / Numerical Single-Choice MCQs** (especially in Physics and Physical Chemistry).
2. **Statement I & Statement II Questions** (*"Both Statement I and II are correct / incorrect..."* — very common in Biology & Inorganic Chemistry).
3. **Assertion (A) & Reason (R) Questions** (*"Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A"* vs *"R is NOT the correct explanation"*).
4. **Match-the-Column (List I with List II)** (tests 4 NCERT facts simultaneously in a single question).
5. **Multi-Statement Selection** (*"How many of the following 5 statements are correct according to NCERT?"*).

## 2. Language Options & Specific Rules for Tamil Nadu Students

### Bilingual Question Paper in Tamil Nadu
- NEET-UG is conducted in **13 languages** nationwide.
- When filling out the NTA online application form, a student selecting exam centers in **Tamil Nadu** (e.g., Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli, Salem, Tirunelveli, Vellore, Erode, Thanjavur, Namakkal, Kanyakumari, etc.) can choose:
  - **English** (Booklet contains English only), OR
  - **Tamil** (Booklet is **Bilingual: Tamil + English printed side-by-side / together**).
- **Crucial NTA Rule:** If a student opts for **Tamil**, she gets **both Tamil and English** in her test booklet! However, NTA stipulates that **in case of any translation ambiguity between Tamil and English, the English version is treated as final and authoritative**.
- **Coach Recommendation:** Even if she is more comfortable thinking in Tamil, she should practice **Bilingual mode (English + Tamil)** so she learns standard English scientific terms (*mitochondria, nucleophilic substitution, torque, glomerular filtration rate*) alongside Tamil explanations.

### Samacheer Kalvi (TN State Board) vs. NCERT Syllabus Bridge
- **NEET Syllabus Authority:** Strictly the **NMC (National Medical Commission) Rationalized Class 11 & Class 12 NCERT Textbooks**.
- **The Trap for Tamil Nadu Students:** Tamil Nadu State Board (*Samacheer Kalvi* SCERT textbooks) actually has slightly *more* descriptive content in Bio-Botany and Bio-Zoology, **but** NEET Biology and Inorganic Chemistry test **exact NCERT wording, NCERT diagram labels, NCERT summary paragraphs, and NCERT tables**.
- **Action Rule:** Even if she studies in a TN State Board school, her primary NEET Bible for Biology and Chemistry **must** be the **official NCERT Class 11 & 12 textbooks** (available in English, and translated Tamil NCERT editions via Tamil Nadu Textbook Corporation / DIKSHA).

## 3. Tamil Nadu Medical Admissions, Quotas & Score Benchmarks

Tamil Nadu has one of the strongest public medical college networks in India (**36+ Government Medical Colleges** plus ESIC KK Nagar Chennai, AIIMS Madurai, and JIPMER Puducherry).

### Two Counseling Pathways for a Tamil Nadu Girl
1. **85% Tamil Nadu State Quota** — Conducted by the **Selection Committee, Directorate of Medical Education and Research (DMER), Chennai** (`https://tnmedicalselection.net`).
   - Uses Tamil Nadu's State Community Reservation matrix:
     - **OC (Open Competition):** 31%
     - **BC (Backward Class):** 26.5%
     - **BCM (Backward Class Muslim):** 3.5%
     - **MBC & DNC (Most Backward Class / Denotified Communities):** 20%
     - **SC (Scheduled Caste):** 15% | **SCA:** 3% | **ST:** 1%
   - **Landmark 7.5% Preferential Government School Quota:** Students who studied **Class 6 to Class 12 continuously in Tamil Nadu State Government Schools** receive a **7.5% horizontal reservation** across all MBBS/BDS seats in TN (with **100% government sponsorship of tuition, hostel, and book fees**).
2. **15% All India Quota (AIQ) & Central Institutes** — Conducted by **MCC (`https://mcc.nic.in`)** for AIIMS Madurai, JIPMER Puducherry (which also has Puducherry domicile seats), and 15% AIQ seats across India.

### Target NEET Score Benchmarks (Out of 720) for Tamil Nadu Government MBBS
*(Cutoffs fluctuate slightly each year based on paper difficulty; aiming +20 marks above historical cutoffs provides a safe margin.)*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

**The Golden Formula for 650+ Marks:**
- **Biology (Botany + Zoology = 360 Marks):** Target **340–355 / 360** (Biology is 50% of the paper and the fastest section — solved in 45–50 minutes).
- **Chemistry (180 Marks):** Target **155–165 / 180** (solved in 50–55 minutes).
- **Physics (180 Marks):** Target **145–160 / 180** (solved in 65–70 minutes, leaving 10–15 minutes buffer for OMR bubbling verification).

## 7. The 3-Round Exam Hall Strategy (How Toppers Avoid -1 Negative Marks)

1. **Sequence:** **Biology (45–50 min)** → **Chemistry (50 min)** → **Physics (65 min)** → **15 min Buffer**.
2. **Bubble OMR Subject-by-Subject** (never wait until minute 175 to bubble all 180 circles at once — one row shift ruins an entire section!).
3. **Round 1 (Sure-Shot +4):** Answer every question you are 100% confident about immediately.
4. **Round 2 (50-50 Elimination & Longer Numericals):** Return to Marked-for-Review questions where you eliminated 2 options or needed calculation time.
5. **Strict Anti-Guessing Rule:** Never blind-guess when all 4 options look unfamiliar. Leaving 5 tough questions blank costs `0` marks (`700/720`); guessing 5 wrong costs `-5` extra marks on top of the missed `20` (`695/720`), which can shift Tamil Nadu State Rank by 400–800 places!
