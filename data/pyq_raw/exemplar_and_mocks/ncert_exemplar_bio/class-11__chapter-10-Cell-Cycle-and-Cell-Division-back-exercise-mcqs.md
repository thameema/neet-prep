## NEET Level 4 Analysis-Based MCQs: Cell Cycle and Cell Division

**Question 1: (Process Analysis)**  A cell undergoing meiosis I is observed to have chiasmata present between homologous chromosomes. Which of the following stages is this cell MOST likely to be in?

A) Prophase I
B) Metaphase I
C) Anaphase I
D) Telophase I

**Correct Answer: A**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 10, Meiosis section; description of Prophase I including crossing over.

**Question 2: (Comparative Analysis)**  Compare the cytokinesis in plant and animal cells. Which statement is INCORRECT regarding the differences?

A) Plant cells form a cell plate, while animal cells form a cleavage furrow.
B) Plant cell cytokinesis involves the Golgi apparatus, while animal cell cytokinesis does not.
C)  Both processes result in the equal division of cytoplasm between daughter cells.
D) Plant cell cytokinesis is centrifugal, while animal cell cytokinesis is centripetal.

**Correct Answer: C**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 10, Cytokinesis section.  Focuses on the differences in mechanisms and the unequal distribution of cytoplasm in some plant cells.

**Question 3: (Cause-Effect Analysis)** If a cell fails to complete the S phase of the cell cycle, what is the MOST likely consequence?

A) The cell will proceed directly to mitosis.
B) The cell will have half the normal amount of DNA in the daughter cells.
C) The cell will arrest in G2 phase.
D) The cell will undergo apoptosis immediately.

**Correct Answer: B**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 10, S phase description and consequences of its failure.

**Question 4: (Exception Analysis)** Which of the following cell types is an exception to the usual rule that cells divide by mitosis?

A) Skin cells
B) Liver cells
C) Nerve cells
D) Intestinal epithelial cells

**Correct Answer: C**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 10; mentions that nerve cells generally do not divide after differentiation.

**Question 5: (Application Analysis)** A researcher is studying a new drug that inhibits the formation of the mitotic spindle.  What stage of the cell cycle would be MOST affected by this drug?

A) G1 phase
B) S phase
C) G2 phase
D) M phase

**Correct Answer: D**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 10, Mitosis section; role of mitotic spindle in chromosome segregation.

**Question 6: (Relationship Analysis)**  What is the relationship between cyclins and cyclin-dependent kinases (CDKs) in regulating the cell cycle?

A) Cyclins activate CDKs, which then phosphorylate target proteins to regulate cell cycle progression.
B) CDKs activate cyclins, which then inhibit cell cycle progression.
C) Cyclins and CDKs act independently of each other.
D) Cyclins inhibit CDKs, preventing cell cycle progression.

**Correct Answer: A**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 10; the regulation of the cell cycle by cyclins and CDKs.

**Question 7: (Conceptual Reasoning)**  A cell with 2n=46 chromosomes undergoes meiosis. How many chromosomes will each daughter cell have at the end of meiosis II?

A) 23
B) 46
C) 92
D) 184

**Correct Answer: A**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 10, Meiosis section; reductional division in meiosis I and equational division in meiosis II.

**Question 8: (Data Interpretation)**  Imagine a cell culture experiment where the number of cells doubles every 24 hours. If you start with 100 cells, approximately how many cells will you have after 72 hours?

A) 200
B) 400
C) 800
D) 1000

**Correct Answer: C**
**NCERT Reference:**  Chapter 10; implicit understanding of exponential growth in cell populations.
