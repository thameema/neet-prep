**Question 1:**  A cell is observed under a microscope exhibiting a prominent nucleolus and extensive RER. Which of the following is the MOST likely function of this cell?

**Question 2:**  A researcher observes two cells, one with a large central vacuole and another with many small vacuoles.  Based solely on this observation, which of the following inferences is MOST likely accurate?

A) Both cells are plant cells.
B) The cell with the large central vacuole is likely a mature plant cell.
C) The cell with many small vacuoles is likely an animal cell.
D) The vacuole size is unrelated to cell type or function.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Question 3:**  Considering the fluid mosaic model of the plasma membrane, which of the following would MOST readily diffuse across the membrane without the aid of transport proteins?

A)  A large, hydrophilic protein.
B)  A small, polar molecule like glucose.
C)  A small, nonpolar molecule like oxygen.
D)  A charged ion like potassium.

**Correct Answer: C**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 8, Cell: The Unit of Life (Plasma Membrane Structure)

**Question 4:**  A cell is treated with a drug that inhibits the function of ribosomes. Which of the following cellular processes would be MOST directly and severely affected?

A)  Active transport across the plasma membrane.
B)  Protein synthesis.
C)  DNA replication.
D)  Cell division (cytokinesis).

**Correct Answer: B**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 8, Cell: The Unit of Life (Ribosome function)

**Question 5:**  A plant cell is placed in a hypotonic solution.  Which of the following BEST describes the subsequent changes in the cell?

A) The cell will plasmolyze.
B) The cell will remain unchanged.
C) The cell will become turgid.
D) The cell will undergo apoptosis.

**Correct Answer: C**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 8, Cell: The Unit of Life (Osmosis and Plant Cells)

**Question 6:**  Comparing prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, which statement is MOST accurate regarding their genetic material?

A) Both have their DNA enclosed within a membrane-bound nucleus.
B) Prokaryotes have linear DNA, while eukaryotes have circular DNA.
C) Eukaryotes have their DNA organized into chromosomes within a nucleus.
D) Both lack membrane-bound organelles.

**Correct Answer: C**
**NCERT Reference:** Chapter 8, Cell: The Unit of Life (Prokaryotic vs. Eukaryotic Cells)


**Question 7:**  Lysosomes are known to contain hydrolytic enzymes.  What would MOST likely happen if the lysosomal membrane were to rupture within a cell?

D) Increased cell growth.
