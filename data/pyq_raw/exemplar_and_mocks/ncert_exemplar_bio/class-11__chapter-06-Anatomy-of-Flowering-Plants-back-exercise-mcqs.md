A)  Formation of vascular cambium → secondary xylem formation towards the inside → secondary phloem formation towards the outside → widening of the vascular cylinder.
B)  Formation of cork cambium → secondary xylem formation towards the outside → secondary phloem formation towards the inside → formation of periderm.
