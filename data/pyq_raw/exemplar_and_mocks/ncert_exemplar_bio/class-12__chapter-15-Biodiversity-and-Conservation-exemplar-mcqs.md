**Question 1:**  A stable community is characterized by which of the following?
A) High species richness and evenness, along with resistance to disturbances.
B) Predominance of a few highly competitive species.
C) Constant fluctuations in species composition and abundance.
D) Low primary productivity and high nutrient cycling.
**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1, SA 1**

**Question 2:** Which of the following scenarios is LEAST likely to have triggered past mass extinctions?
A) Asteroid impact causing widespread environmental changes.
B) Gradual climate change over millions of years.
C) A sudden increase in global biodiversity.
D) Massive volcanic eruptions releasing toxic gases.
**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 2, SA 2**

**Question 3:** India's exceptional ecological diversity is primarily attributed to:
A) Uniform climate and habitat types across the country.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 3, SA 3**

**Question 4:** A high-productivity man-made ecosystem with very low diversity is best exemplified by:
