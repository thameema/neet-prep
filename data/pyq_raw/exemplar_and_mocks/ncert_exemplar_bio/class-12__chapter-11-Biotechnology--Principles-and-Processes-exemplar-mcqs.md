**Question 1:**  A plasmid vector with a high copy number is used for recombinant protein production.  Which of the following BEST explains the advantage of a high copy number?

A) Increased chances of successful transformation.
B) Higher yield of recombinant protein per cell.
C) Reduced risk of plasmid degradation.
D) Enhanced expression of the antibiotic resistance gene.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1, SA 1**


**Question 2:** During recombinant DNA technology, which enzyme would be LEAST suitable for creating blunt ends?

A) EcoRI
B) HindIII
C) SmaI
D) BamHI

**Exemplar Reference: VSA 2, VSA 4**


**Question 3:** The 'H' in HindIII refers to:
