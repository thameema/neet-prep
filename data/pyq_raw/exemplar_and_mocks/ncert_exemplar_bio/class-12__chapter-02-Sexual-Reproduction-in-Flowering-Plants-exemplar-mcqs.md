**Question 1:**  The egg apparatus is crucial for fertilization.  Which combination correctly identifies its components and their respective roles?

A) Egg cell (syngamy), synergids (pollen tube guidance), antipodals (nutrient supply).
B) Egg cell (syngamy), synergids (pollen tube guidance), central cell (endosperm formation).
C) Egg cell (endosperm formation), synergids (pollen tube guidance), antipodals (syngamy).
D) Egg cell (syngamy), synergids (nutrient supply), central cell (pollen tube guidance).

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1, SA 1**


**Question 2:**  A pollen grain lands on a stigma.  What specific gynoecium component ultimately determines if fertilization will occur?

A) Style, due to its role in pollen tube growth.
B) Ovary, as it houses the ovules.
C) Stigma, based on its surface chemistry and recognition molecules.
D) Placenta, because it nourishes the developing ovules.

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 2, SA 2**


**Question 3:**  Both cotyledons and the nucellus provide nourishment.  However, their sources of nutrients differ significantly. What's the key difference?

A) Cotyledons derive nutrients from the endosperm, while the nucellus derives nutrients from the ovule's integuments.
B) Cotyledons derive nutrients from the endosperm, while the nucellus derives nutrients from the parent sporophyte.
C) Cotyledons derive nutrients directly from the parent sporophyte, while the nucellus derives nutrients from the endosperm.
D) Both derive nutrients equally from the endosperm.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 3, SA 3**


**Question 4:**  During microsporogenesis, which cellular process is responsible for the transition from a pollen mother cell to a pollen tetrad?

A) Mitosis, resulting in genetically identical pollen grains.
B) Meiosis I, followed by Meiosis II, resulting in genetically diverse microspores.
C) Mitosis, followed by cytokinesis, resulting in four genetically identical pollen grains.
D) Meiosis I only, producing two genetically diverse cells that undergo further division.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 4, SA 4**


**Question 5:** In the development of the female gametophyte, which stages involve meiosis and mitosis respectively?

A) Meiosis occurs in stage 1 (megaspore mother cell to megaspores), mitosis in stages 2 and 3 (megaspores to embryo sac and egg).
B) Mitosis occurs in stage 1, meiosis in stages 2 and 3.
C) Meiosis occurs in stages 1 and 2, mitosis in stage 3.
D) Mitosis occurs in stage 1, meiosis in stage 2, and mitosis in stage 3.

**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 5, SA 5**


**Question 6:**  A pollen tube successfully navigates the style and enters the ovule. Which structure does it penetrate *immediately* before reaching the egg apparatus?

**Question 7:**  Following successful fertilization, what specific parts of the pistil develop into the fruit and seed respectively?
