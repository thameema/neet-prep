**Question 1:**  Which of the following characteristics is LEAST likely to be preserved in a fossil?

A) Hard skeletal structures
B)  Soft tissues like muscles
C)  Original organic molecules
D)  Overall morphology (shape and structure)

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1, SA 1**

**Question 2:**  A fossil of an aquatic organism is found in a sedimentary rock layer.  Which of the following inferences is MOST likely to be accurate?

A) The organism lived in a shallow marine environment.
B) The organism lived only in deep ocean trenches.
C) The organism lived in a terrestrial environment.
D) The location of the fossil provides no information about the organism's habitat.

**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 2, SA 2**

**Question 3:** The term "complex organism," in the context of evolutionary biology, primarily refers to:

A) Organisms with a high degree of cellular differentiation.
B) Organisms with a large number of cells.
C) Organisms exhibiting a high level of genetic diversity.
D) Organisms with a large body size.

**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 3, SA 3**


**Question 4:**  A scientist wants to determine the age of a recently felled tree.  Which method would be MOST reliable?

A) Radiocarbon dating of the wood.
B) Counting the annual growth rings.
C) Analyzing the isotopic composition of the leaves.
D) Examining the tree's overall size and shape.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 4, SA 4**

**Question 5:** Convergent evolution is best illustrated by which of the following pairs, with respect to the feature they share?

A)  The wings of a bird and the wings of a butterfly – powered flight
B)  The flippers of a whale and the fins of a shark – streamlined body shape
C)  The eyes of an octopus and the eyes of a human – image formation
D) All of the above show convergent evolution.

**Correct Answer: D**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 5, SA 5**


**Question 6:**  Which technique would be MOST accurate for determining the age of a newly discovered fossil embedded in volcanic rock?

A) Radiocarbon dating
B) Potassium-Argon dating
C) Dendrochronology
D) Stratigraphic analysis alone

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 6, SA 6**


**Question 7:**  Adaptive radiation is MOST likely to occur when:

A)  A population experiences a sudden environmental change.
B)  A population faces intense competition from other species.
C)  A population colonizes a new environment with diverse, unoccupied niches.
D)  A population undergoes a bottleneck event.

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 7, SA 7**

**Question 8:**  The age of a sedimentary rock layer containing fossils can BEST be determined by:

A) Radiocarbon dating of the fossils.
B)  Analyzing the relative positions of the rock layers (stratigraphy).
C)  Potassium-argon dating of the surrounding igneous rocks.
D) Both B and C.

**Correct Answer: D**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 8, SA 8**

**Question 9:** Functional macromolecules like enzymes are primarily evolving towards:

A) Increased size and complexity.
B)  Greater catalytic efficiency and specificity.
C)  Decreased energy requirements.
D)  Reduced susceptibility to degradation.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 9, SA 9**

**Question 10:** In a population with the allele frequencies of B = 0.5 and b = 0.5, what would be the expected genotype frequencies under Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium?

A) BB = 0.25, Bb = 0.5, bb = 0.25
B) BB = 0.75, Bb = 0.25, bb = 0
C) BB = 0.5, Bb = 0.5, bb = 0
D) BB = 0, Bb = 1, bb = 0

**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 10, SA 10**

**Question 11:**  The appearance of antibiotic-resistant bacteria is best explained by:

A)  Adaptive radiation leading to new species.
B)  Horizontal gene transfer introducing pre-existing resistance.
C)  Divergent evolution creating new bacterial forms.
D)  Transduction resulting in genetic recombination.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: LA 8**


**Question 12:**  The evolutionary trend of life moving from water to land is primarily evidenced by:

A) The fossil record of amphibians evolving from fish-like ancestors.

D) The development of aquatic adaptations in terrestrial animals.

**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: LA 9**

**Question 13:** Viviparity is considered an evolutionary advantage mainly because:

**Question 14:** Fossils are most commonly found in:

**Question 20:**  Considering the process of fossilization, which of the following factors would MOST significantly hinder the formation of a complete fossil?

**Question 21:** A paleontologist discovers a fossil in a layer of rock known to be 100 million years old.  What method was MOST likely used to determine the age of the rock layer?

A) Radiocarbon dating
B) Dendrochronology

**Question 22:**  Why is the concept of "unoccupied niches" crucial for adaptive radiation?

A) It reduces competition for resources.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: SA7**

**Question 23:** How does the Hardy-Weinberg principle help us understand evolution?
