**Quick Analysis MCQs (based on VSA):**

**Question 1:**  Which sequence correctly represents the pathway of an electrical impulse through a single neuron?
A) Dendrites → Cell body → Axon → Axon terminal → Synaptic knob
B) Axon terminal → Synaptic knob → Dendrites → Cell body → Axon
C) Cell body → Dendrites → Axon → Axon terminal → Synaptic knob
D) Synaptic knob → Axon terminal → Axon → Cell body → Dendrites
**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1**

**Question 2:**  During resting potential, the axonal membrane is polarized.  Which ion movement directly contributes to this polarization?
A)  Influx of Na+, efflux of K+
B)  Efflux of Na+, influx of K+
C)  Influx of both Na+ and K+
D)  Efflux of both Na+ and K+
**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 5**

**Question 3:** Which retinal cells are primarily responsible for distinguishing the color of a red apple?
A) Rods
B) Cones
C) Bipolar cells
D) Ganglion cells
**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 3**

**Question 4:** The correct sequence of structures involved in sound wave transmission from the external ear to the cochlea is:
A) External auditory canal → ear drum → malleus → incus → stapes → cochlea → cochlear nerve
B) Ear drum → external auditory canal → malleus → incus → stapes → cochlea → cochlear nerve
C) External auditory canal → ear drum → stapes → incus → malleus → cochlea → cochlear nerve
D) Cochlear nerve → cochlea → stapes → incus → malleus → ear drum → external auditory canal
**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 4**

**Question 5:**  Which structure is NOT directly involved in the protection of the brain?

**Question 8:** What is the primary difference between electrical and chemical transmission of nerve impulses?
A) Electrical transmission is faster and occurs over longer distances; chemical transmission is slower and localized.
B) Electrical transmission uses neurotransmitters; chemical transmission uses ion channels.
C) Electrical transmission occurs across synapses; chemical transmission occurs along axons.
D) Electrical transmission is unidirectional; chemical transmission is bidirectional.

**Question 16:**  A neurotoxin blocks voltage-gated sodium channels in axons.  What would be the immediate effect on nerve impulse transmission?
A) Increased speed of impulse transmission
B)  No effect on impulse transmission

D)  Blockage of action potential generation
**Correct Answer: D**


**Question 17:** Comparing the roles of rods and cones in vision, which statement is TRUE?
A) Rods are responsible for color vision; cones are responsible for low-light vision.
B) Cones are responsible for color vision; rods are responsible for low-light vision.
C) Both rods and cones are equally sensitive to all wavelengths of light.
D) Rods and cones are not involved in vision.
**Correct Answer: B**

**Question 18:** Which of the following is NOT a function of the cerebrospinal fluid?
A) Shock absorption
B) Nutrient transport
C)  Impulse transmission
D)  Waste removal
**Correct Answer: C**

**Question 19:** The myelin sheath significantly increases the speed of nerve impulse transmission by:
A)  Decreasing the resistance of the axon membrane.
B)  Increasing the capacitance of the axon membrane.
C)  Facilitating saltatory conduction.
D)  Slowing down the rate of repolarization.
**Correct Answer: C**

**Question 20:**  Which part of the ear is responsible for the detection of linear acceleration?

**Question 21:**  Which of the following statements about the synapse is FALSE?

B)  The synapse is a unidirectional pathway.
C)  Electrical synapses are faster than chemical synapses.
D)  Synaptic transmission always involves neurotransmitters.
**Correct Answer: D** (Electrical synapses exist)

**Question 22:**  The process of converting sound waves into nerve impulses occurs in which structure?

**Question 24:** What is the role of the meninges in protecting the brain?
