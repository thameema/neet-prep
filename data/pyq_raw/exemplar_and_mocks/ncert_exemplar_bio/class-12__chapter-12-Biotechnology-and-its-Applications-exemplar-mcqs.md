**VSA-Based Quick Analysis MCQs:**

**Question 1:** Considering the limitations of the Green Revolution, which of the following is NOT a direct consequence of excessive fertilizer use?
A) Soil degradation and nutrient depletion
B) Increased water pollution due to runoff
C) Enhanced biodiversity in agricultural ecosystems
D) Development of pesticide-resistant pests
**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 1**

**Question 2:**  The "Green Revolution" primarily focused on:
A)  Sustainable agricultural practices minimizing environmental impact.
B)  Increased crop yields through high-yielding varieties and chemical inputs.
C)  Organic farming techniques emphasizing biodiversity.
D)  Traditional farming methods relying on natural resources.
**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 1**

**Question 3:** Which of the following is a direct consequence of the Green Revolution's emphasis on monoculture?
A) Increased genetic diversity in crops.
B) Enhanced resilience to pests and diseases.
C) Increased susceptibility to widespread crop failure.
D) Reduced need for pesticides and herbicides.
**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 1**

**Question 4:**  A GMO differs from a hybrid primarily because:
A) GMOs involve cross-breeding of closely related species.
B) GMOs introduce foreign genes into the organism's genome.
C) Hybrids always exhibit increased yield compared to GMOs.
D) GMOs are only developed in plants, while hybrids are used in animals.
**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 2**


**SA-Based Moderate Analysis MCQs:**

**Question 5:**  A farmer uses a new high-yielding rice variety developed through genetic modification.  However, he observes decreased yields after several seasons.  Which is the MOST likely explanation?
A) The new variety is inherently unstable.
B) The new variety has developed resistance to all pesticides.
C) The pest population has evolved resistance to the pesticides used.
D) The farmer failed to use sufficient fertilizers.
**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 1, 8**

**Question 6:** ELISA is used for detecting diseases because it:
A) Directly visualizes the pathogen.
B) Amplifies the pathogen's DNA for easy detection.
C) Detects the presence of antibodies or antigens specific to the pathogen.
D) Measures the level of antibiotics in the patient's blood.
**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 4**

**Question 7:** Biopiracy primarily exploits which aspect of developing countries?
A) Their advanced biotechnology infrastructure.
B) Their traditional knowledge of medicinal plants and other resources.
C) Their high levels of industrialization.
D) Their strong patent laws protecting indigenous resources.
**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 6**

**Question 8:** The secretion of inactive toxic proteins by microorganisms is advantageous because:
A) It allows for easy transport of the toxin.
B) It prevents self-poisoning of the producing organism.
C) It enhances the toxin's stability in the environment.
D) It makes the toxin more effective against its target.
**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 7**

**Question 9:**  A new diagnostic test detects a disease before symptoms appear. Which principle is MOST likely involved?
A) ELISA
B) PCR
C) Antibody detection in blood
D) Presence of pathogens in body fluids
**Correct Answer: B (PCR can detect even minuscule amounts of pathogen DNA before symptoms)**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 5**

**Question 10:** Why did the Indian Parliament pass the second amendment to the country's patents bill?  A primary reason was to:
A)  Strengthen the intellectual property rights of foreign companies.
B)  Better protect the rights of Indian inventors and traditional knowledge.
C)  Eliminate patent protection for all medicinal plants.
D)  Allow unrestricted access to all genetic resources.
**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 9**


**LA-Based Deep Analysis MCQs:**

**Question 11:**  Explain how genetic modification, while offering benefits, also poses long-term risks by disregarding genetic barriers.  Which of the following is NOT a significant long-term risk?
A) Unforeseen ecological consequences due to gene flow.
B) Development of new, more virulent pathogens.
C) Increased crop yields in all environments.
D) Potential for allergic reactions in humans consuming modified crops.
**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 8**

**Question 12:** Compare and contrast diagnostics and therapeutics in biotechnology, providing examples of each. Which statement is INCORRECT?
A) Diagnostics involve identifying a disease, while therapeutics involve treating it.
B) ELISA is a diagnostic tool, while gene therapy is a therapeutic application.
C) Diagnostics always precede therapeutics in disease management.
D) Both diagnostics and therapeutics rely on understanding biological processes.
**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 3**

**Question 13:** Analyze the ethical considerations surrounding biopiracy, focusing on the exploitation of developing countries.  Which of the following is NOT a central ethical concern?
A) Lack of benefit-sharing with the source communities.
B) Loss of traditional knowledge and biodiversity.
C) Strict enforcement of intellectual property rights by developed nations.
D) Increased economic development in developing countries.
**Correct Answer: D**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 6**

**Question 14:**  Discuss the role of biotechnology in addressing the current food crisis.  Which of the following is NOT a major contribution of biotechnology to increasing food security?
A) Development of high-yielding crop varieties.
B) Pest and disease-resistant crops.
C) Enhanced nutritional value in crops.
D) Complete elimination of the need for pesticides.
**Correct Answer: D**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 1**

**Question 15:**  Explain how the limitations of the Green Revolution necessitate a new approach to agricultural practices. Which of the following is NOT a key element needed for a sustainable solution to the food crisis?
A)  Emphasis on genetically modified crops.
B)  Conservation of biodiversity.
C)  Sustainable water management.
D)  Reduced reliance on chemical inputs.
**Correct Answer: A (While GMOs can be part of the solution, over-reliance is unsustainable)**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 1**
