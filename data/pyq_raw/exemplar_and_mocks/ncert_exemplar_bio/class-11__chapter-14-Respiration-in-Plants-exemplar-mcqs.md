**Question 1:** During respiration, energy released from the oxidation of glucose is primarily stored in which form, and how is it subsequently released for cellular processes?

A)  Stored as heat energy; released directly as heat for cellular work.
B)  Stored as ATP; released through hydrolysis of ATP to ADP and inorganic phosphate.
C)  Stored as NADH; released through electron transport chain.
D) Stored as FADH2; released through substrate-level phosphorylation.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1, SA 1**

**Question 2:**  What is the primary role of ATP in living organisms, and which molecule serves this function in both plants and animals?

A)  ATP acts as a structural component of cell membranes.
B)  ATP acts as an energy currency, transferring energy from energy-releasing reactions to energy-requiring reactions; ATP is the energy currency in both plants and animals.
C)  ATP acts as a genetic messenger, carrying information from the nucleus to the cytoplasm.
D) ATP acts as a catalyst, speeding up biochemical reactions;  NADH serves this function in both.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 2, SA 2**

**Question 3:**  A plant tissue sample exhibits an RQ of 0.7.  Which substrate is most likely being oxidized?

**Question 4:**  If the respiratory quotient (RQ) is less than 1, what does this indicate about the type of respiratory substrate being utilized, and what is the relationship between CO2 produced and O2 consumed in this scenario?

A) Carbohydrates are being oxidized; CO2 produced is equal to O2 consumed.
B) Fats or proteins are being oxidized; CO2 produced is less than O2 consumed.
C) Proteins are being oxidized; CO2 produced is greater than O2 consumed.
D) Carbohydrates are being oxidized; CO2 produced is greater than O2 consumed.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 3, SA 3**


**Question 5:** During anaerobic respiration in humans, what is the final product of glycolysis, and what molecule is crucial in facilitating this process?

**Question 6:**  Which of the following will release the MOST energy upon complete oxidation, and why?
