**Question 1:** Millions of chickens were killed in several Indian states due to a highly contagious disease.  Which of the following best explains the primary reason for this mass culling?

A) To prevent the spread of a viral disease like Avian Influenza, minimizing economic losses and protecting human health.
B) To control the population of chickens to maintain market equilibrium.
C) Due to a widespread outbreak of a bacterial infection easily treatable with antibiotics.
D) As a precautionary measure to prevent potential cross-contamination with other poultry species.

**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA/SA Question 1**


**Question 2:**  A farmer wants to introduce a new rice variety that allows for multiple harvests per year. Which agronomic characteristic is MOST crucial for achieving this?

A) Disease resistance, ensuring minimal crop loss between harvests.
B) Shorter plant height, enabling denser planting and efficient resource utilization.
C) Early yielding variety, allowing for faster crop cycles and multiple harvests within a year.
D) Superior irrigation requirements, ensuring consistent water supply for rapid growth.

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: LA Question 11**


**Question 3:** Gamma rays are used in mutation breeding for crop improvement. However, this technique presents a potential risk. Which of the following best describes this risk?

A) Gamma rays enhance the nutritional value of crops, making them less palatable.
B) The process is cost-prohibitive and not feasible for widespread application.
C)  Exposure to gamma rays during the process might pose health hazards to humans and the environment.
D) Gamma rays can cause unpredictable genetic changes, potentially leading to undesirable traits.


**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: SA Question 2**


**Question 4:**  Two closely related animal breeds are crossed in animal husbandry.  What is the MOST likely outcome of this inbreeding process concerning the genetic makeup of the offspring?

A) Increased heterozygosity and hybrid vigor.
B) Increased homozygosity and potential for expression of recessive deleterious genes.
C) Enhanced disease resistance due to increased genetic diversity.
D) Improved overall fitness and adaptability to various environments.


**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: LA Question 3**


**Question 5:**  Which of the following plant diseases is NOT caused by a fungus?

A) Rust of wheat
B) Smut of bajra
C) Black rot of crucifers
D) Red rot of sugarcane


**Correct Answer:  None of the above (All are fungal diseases according to the exemplar)**
**Exemplar Reference: LA Question 10**


**Question 6:**  In virus-infected plants, meristematic tissues remain virus-free.  Which statement BEST explains this phenomenon?

A) Meristem cells are inherently resistant to viral infection.
B) Meristems contain antiviral compounds that neutralize the virus.
C) The rapid cell division rate in meristems outpaces viral replication.
D) Viruses are unable to penetrate the protective layers surrounding meristematic cells.


**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: LA Question 10**


**Question 7:** A sugarcane farmer aims for optimal yield. Which combination of traits would be MOST desirable?

A) Thick stem, long internodes, high sugar content, and disease resistance.
B) Thick stem, short internodes, low sugar content, and disease resistance.
C) Thin stem, long internodes, high sugar content, and susceptibility to diseases.
D) Thick stem, long internodes, low sugar content, and disease resistance.


**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: LA Question 9**


**Question 8:** Biofortification is a scientific process aimed at:

A) Enhancing crop protection through pesticide use.
B) Improving the nutritional content of crops.
C) Developing disease-resistant crop varieties.
D) Increasing crop yield through advanced breeding techniques.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: LA Question 11**


**Question 9:** Totipotency describes the ability of a:

A) Seed to germinate and grow into a plant.
B) Bud to develop into a complete plant.
C) Cell to differentiate into various plant tissues.
D) Single cell to develop into a whole plant.

**Correct Answer: D**
**Exemplar Reference: LA Question 12**


**Question 10:**  Fungicides and antibiotics primarily function by:

A) Enhancing yield and disease resistance in crops.
B) Killing all types of pathogenic microbes.
C) Killing pathogenic fungi and bacteria, respectively.
D) Killing pathogenic bacteria and fungi, respectively.

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: LA Question 9**


**Question 11:**  Mutation breeding involves:

A) Recombinant DNA technology to introduce new genes.
B) Using chemicals or radiation to alter gene sequences.
C) Gene therapy to correct genetic defects in crops.
D) Transgenic mechanisms to transfer genes from one species to another.

**Correct Answer: B**
