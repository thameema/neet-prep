**Question 1:**  Smaller, lipid-soluble molecules diffuse faster across the cell membrane.  However, hydrophilic substances require facilitated transport.  Which statement BEST explains the difference in transport mechanisms?

A) Lipid-soluble molecules passively dissolve into the membrane bilayer, while hydrophilic molecules require protein channels or carriers.
B) Both types of molecules utilize protein channels, but lipid-soluble molecules bind more strongly to the channel proteins.
C) Hydrophilic molecules are actively transported against their concentration gradient, while lipid-soluble molecules move passively down their gradient.
D) The cell membrane is selectively permeable only to hydrophilic molecules, requiring active transport for lipid-soluble substances.

**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1, SA 1**


**Question 2:**  In passive transport, two different molecules move in opposite directions independently. This process is BEST described as:

A) Active transport
B) Uniport
C) Antiport
D) Symport

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 2, SA 2**


**Question 3:** The rate and direction of osmosis across a cell membrane are primarily determined by:

A) The concentration of solutes inside the cell only.
B) The concentration of water molecules inside and outside the cell.
C) The water potential gradient across the membrane.
D) The permeability of the cell membrane to solutes only.

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 3, SA 3**


**Question 4:** A plant wilts and dies after urea is added to the soil. This is MOST likely due to:

A) Increased water potential in the soil, leading to reduced water uptake.
B) Urea toxicity causing damage to root cells.
C)  A decrease in soil osmotic potential, leading to plasmolysis.
D)  Stimulation of excessive transpiration.

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 4, SA 4**


**Question 5:** Imbibition in dry seeds primarily increases which property, facilitating seedling emergence?

A) Water potential
B) Turgor pressure
C) Osmotic pressure
D) Cell membrane fluidity

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 5, SA 5**


**Question 6:** The rapid upward movement of water in tall trees is primarily due to:

A) Root pressure alone
B) Transpiration pull
C) Diffusion
D) Cytoplasmic streaming

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 6, SA 6**


**Question 7:**  Specificity of transport across plant cells primarily occurs at the cell membrane rather than the cell wall because:

A) The cell wall is freely permeable to all substances.
B) The cell membrane possesses selective transport proteins.
C) The cell wall is only involved in structural support.
D) Both A and B

**Correct Answer: D**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 7, SA 7**


**Question 8:** Which process BEST exemplifies imbibition?

A) Uptake of water by root hairs.
B) Gas exchange in stomata.
C) Swelling of a seed in soil.

**Question 9:** During senescence, nutrients in plant leaves are primarily:

A) Accumulated
B) Withdrawn

**Question 10:** The water potential of pure water at standard temperature is:

A) -20 MPa
B) +20 MPa
C) Zero

**Question 11:** Mycorrhizae benefit plants primarily by enhancing:

A) Only water absorption.

**Question 12:** Referring to a simple diagram showing osmosis across a semi-permeable membrane (like the one in Exemplar LA 10), which statement is INCORRECT?
