**Question 1:**  An earthworm's clitellum, a prominent dark band, plays a crucial role in reproduction. Considering its location spanning segments 14-16, which of the following statements BEST explains its functional significance?

A)  The clitellum secretes mucus to aid in locomotion, facilitating sperm exchange between earthworms.
B) The clitellum's position ensures the cocoon formation encloses fertilized eggs, protecting them during development.
C) The clitellum's dark pigmentation attracts mates, enhancing reproductive success through visual cues.
D) The clitellum's segmented structure allows for synchronized muscle contractions during copulation.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1, SA 1**


**Question 2:** Sclerites, the hardened plates in a cockroach's exoskeleton, provide structural support and protection.  Which of the following BEST describes their arrangement and functional consequence?

A) Sclerites are arranged randomly, providing flexible movement but limited protection.
B) Sclerites are fused together forming a rigid exoskeleton, limiting movement but offering robust protection.
C) Sclerites are articulated, allowing for flexible movement while maintaining protection.
D) Sclerites are located only on the head, providing protection for vital organs.

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 2, SA 2**
