**Question 1:** Mannitol, a storage carbohydrate, is characteristic of which algal group?  Considering the diverse storage mechanisms in algae, which statement BEST explains the evolutionary advantage of mannitol storage in this specific group?

A) Phaeophyceae; Mannitol's solubility aids in rapid mobilization of energy during periods of fluctuating light intensity.
B) Rhodophyceae; Mannitol's structural similarity to starch allows efficient enzymatic conversion.
C) Chlorophyceae; Mannitol's high energy density maximizes energy storage per unit volume.
D)  Bacillariophyceae; Mannitol's resistance to degradation ensures long-term energy reserves.

**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1, SA 1**

**Question 2:**  Which of the following statements accurately compares the rhizoids of bryophytes to the roots of vascular plants?

A) Both structures primarily function in water and nutrient absorption, demonstrating convergent evolution.
B) Bryophyte rhizoids are multicellular and complex, analogous to the root systems of vascular plants.
C)  Bryophyte rhizoids lack vascular tissue and are primarily for anchorage, unlike the complex vascular roots of higher plants.
D) Both structures exhibit similar developmental patterns originating from apical meristems.

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 4**

**Question 3:**  A plant exhibits a dominant gametophyte generation and lacks vascular tissue.  To which group does it MOST likely belong?

A) Pteridophytes
B) Gymnosperms
C) Bryophytes
D) Angiosperms

**Correct Answer: C**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 5, SA 1**

**Question 4:**  Which of the following algae exemplifies a haplo-diplontic life cycle, showcasing both haploid and diploid multicellular stages?  Justify your choice based on the relative dominance of each stage.

A) *Chlamydomonas* (Haplontic)
B) *Polysiphonia* (Haplo-diplontic)
C) *Fucus* (Diplontic)
D) *Spirogyra* (Haplontic)

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 5, SA 4**

**Question 5:**  Why are bryophytes considered the "amphibians of the plant kingdom"?

A) They require water for sexual reproduction and exhibit a terrestrial lifestyle.
B) They possess both aquatic and terrestrial adaptations, bridging the gap between aquatic algae and terrestrial plants.
C) They can survive in both aquatic and terrestrial environments, exhibiting amphibious characteristics.
D) They are primarily aquatic, but can tolerate periods of desiccation, similar to amphibians.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: SA 1**

**Question 6:**  Compare the reproductive structures of a fern (pteridophyte) with those of an angiosperm. Which statement BEST highlights a key difference?

A) Both produce spores, but only angiosperms produce seeds.
B) Ferns have archegonia and antheridia, while angiosperms have ovules and pollen grains.
C) Both utilize wind pollination, but angiosperms have evolved more elaborate mechanisms for pollen dispersal.
D) Ferns exhibit homospory, while angiosperms exhibit heterospory.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Question 7:** Selaginella, a heterosporous pteridophyte, displays characteristics that foreshadow the seed habit.  What is the MOST significant characteristic that Selaginella lacks compared to seed plants?

A) Megaspores and microspores
B)  Retention of the megaspore within the megasporangium
C)  Development of the female gametophyte within the megaspore
D)  Formation of a protective seed coat around the embryo

**Correct Answer: D**
**Exemplar Reference: SA 4, LA 6**

**Question 8:** Heterospory, the production of microspores and megaspores, is considered an evolutionary advancement. What is the PRIMARY advantage conferred by heterospory?

A) Increased genetic diversity through cross-pollination.
B) Enhanced spore dispersal efficiency.
C)  Development of more robust sporophytes.
D) Reduced competition between male and female gametophytes.

**Correct Answer: A**
**Exemplar Reference: SA 3**


**Question 9:**  The prothallus of a fern represents which generation in its life cycle, and what is its primary function?

C) Sporophyte; produces gametes.
D) Gametophyte; produces spores.

**Question 10:**  In a lichen, what would happen if the algal component were separated from the fungal component?
