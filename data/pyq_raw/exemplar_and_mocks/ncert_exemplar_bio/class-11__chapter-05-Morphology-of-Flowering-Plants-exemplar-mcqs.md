**Question 1:** Plants in marshy areas like the Sundarbans develop specialized roots for oxygen uptake.  Which of the following adaptations BEST explains how these roots acquire oxygen for respiration in oxygen-deficient environments?

A)  Increased root hair density for enhanced water absorption, indirectly increasing oxygen availability.
B)  Development of pneumatophores, which extend above the water surface for atmospheric oxygen uptake.
C)  Formation of aerenchyma tissue, creating air channels within the roots for oxygen diffusion.
D)  Increased production of anaerobic enzymes to facilitate respiration in the absence of oxygen.

**Correct Answer: B**
**Exemplar Reference: VSA 1, SA 1**
