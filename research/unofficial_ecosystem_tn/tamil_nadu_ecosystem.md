## 2. 7.5% government school quota and 85% state quota: rules, cut-offs, counselling guides

### Takeaway
The 7.5% horizontal reservation (students who studied classes 6 to 12 in Tamil Nadu government schools and qualified NEET) is intact for 2026-27 with 641 seats and full fee coverage on paper, but it is under a mandatory five-year review. Round 1 2026 government-quota MBBS closing marks in government colleges ran from 577 (OC) to 433 (ST) per one third-party tabulation; reliable category-wise 2024 and 2025 closing marks were not obtained.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Computed 2026 Tamil Nadu pass rate: 61,306 / 1,08,601 = about 56.5%, in line with 2025 (56.13%). So the fall in qualifiers is driven by about 27,000 fewer candidates appearing, not by a lower pass rate.
- Tamil-paper share: the Tamil counts are national registrations, not Tamil Nadu candidates who appeared, so only a rough ratio is possible: about 24% of the 2024 Tamil Nadu appeared count (36,335 / 1,52,920), about 20% in 2025 (26,580 / 1,35,715) and about 27% in 2026 (29,845 / 1,08,601). The numerators and denominators are not the same population.
- 2026 government school conversion: 614 allotted out of 3,625 accepted applicants is about 17% in the first allotment; 641 seats against 3,625 applicants means roughly one in six on the 7.5% list can get an MBBS or BDS seat.
